# `gk-design` (02-design-decks-pages)

Brand Design, Slide Decks & Campaign Pages: multi-size PNG social banners, 16:9 and 4:5 HTML, PDF, and editable PowerPoint slide decks, and standalone conversion landing pages.

- **Plugin Manifest:** [`.claude-plugin/plugin.json`](./.claude-plugin/plugin.json) ([Raw](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/madmancf/octomarketer-skills/main/plugins/02-design-decks-pages/.claude-plugin/plugin.json))
- **Models:** **Claude Opus 5.5** (`claude-opus-5-5`) for strategy, voice, decks, and creative direction; **Claude Sonnet 5.5** (`claude-sonnet-5-5`, `high` effort) for diagnostics, daily drafts, and subagent runs.

## Workflow Commands

- [`/run-deck-or-page`](./commands/run-deck-or-page.md) ([Raw](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/madmancf/octomarketer-skills/main/plugins/02-design-decks-pages/commands/run-deck-or-page.md)): Use when building branded social banner packs, 16:9 HTML/PDF/PPTX slide decks, or lead-magnet and webinar landing pages.
